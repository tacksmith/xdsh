# Code of Conduct

XDSH contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces safe, technical, and
welcoming. Be respectful, assume good faith, discuss the work rather than the
person, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private data or
credentials, and sustained disruptive behavior are not accepted. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban an account when that
is necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private security-advisory channel. Include only the minimum information needed
to investigate, and do not post personal or credential data publicly. Reports
will be handled as confidentially and promptly as the available evidence allows.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, and
project-operated communication channels.
